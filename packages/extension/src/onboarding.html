Ocular

Hello. Watch prices in two clicks.

Local-first price tracker built right into your browser. No account needed, zero tracking, 100% private.

01

Pin Ocular to your toolbar

Click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome, find Ocular, and click the pin icon for 1-click access to your price watchlist.

02

Open a product page

Visit Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Croma, Reliance Digital, Nykaa, Nike, or Decathlon. An Ocular badge will appear automatically.

03

Click "Watch this price"

Click the badge anytime to see full price history graphs, current deals, usual 90-day prices, and custom price drop alerts.

Privacy & Local Storage

Ocular runs checks directly from your browser. Every product page you visit records price observations locally, keeping your history updated without giving away your data.
