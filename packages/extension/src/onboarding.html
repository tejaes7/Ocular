Ocular

Watch a price in two clicks.

No account, no server, no tracking. Everything stays in this browser.

  1. Pin Ocular to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 to the right of the address bar, find Ocular, and hit the pin. That eye icon is your watchlist.

  2. Open a product page

    Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, AJIO, Croma, Reliance Digital, Nykaa, Tata CLiQ or Meesho. A Monitor price button appears at the bottom right.

  3. Press it

    The dot turns green. Click the button any time to see price history, whether automatic checks are working, and to stop watching.

One thing worth knowing

Ocular checks prices from your browser rather than a server. That's what stops retailers from blocking it — but it also means checks run only while your browser is open. Every product page you visit is recorded automatically, so normal browsing keeps your history fresh.
