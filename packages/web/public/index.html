It knows what "usual" means

Ocular compares against the 90-day median, never the crossed-out M.R.P. A price raised for a week and then "discounted" back doesn't fool it.

It checks from your browser

Retailers block servers, not people. Ocular checks from your own browser session, which is why it keeps working where scrapers get captcha'd.

It stays yours

Everything lives in your browser. No sign-up, no analytics, no profile. Export your full history to a file whenever you want.

Install

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
  4. Pin Ocular from the 🧩 menu, then open any product page.
