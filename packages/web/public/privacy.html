Ocular

Privacy

Ocular does not have accounts, does not use analytics, and does not build a profile of you.

What stays on your device

The products you watch and their full price history are stored locally, in your browser's extension storage. They are not transmitted anywhere. Removing the extension deletes them — export a backup first if you want to keep them.

What Ocular requests over the network

To read a price, Ocular requests the product pages you have asked it to watch, from your own browser, using your existing session. It does not send those pages anywhere else.

Optional background sync

Background sync is off by default. If you turn it on and supply your own server address, Ocular sends that server the URLs and titles of the products you watch, plus a randomly generated device identifier. That identifier is not linked to an email address, an account, or anything that identifies you. No prices, settings, or browsing history beyond those product URLs are sent.

Permissions

Ocular requests access only to the retail sites it supports, so it can read prices on product pages you open. It does not read pages on any other site.
