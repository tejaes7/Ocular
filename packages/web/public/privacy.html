Privacy

Last updated 2 August 2026

Ocular does not use analytics and does not build a profile of you. The prices you track are stored on your device. An account is optional — and if you turn on email price alerts, your watchlist becomes linked to it. That link is described in full below, and you can undo it.

What stays on your device

The products you watch and their full price history are stored locally, in your browser's extension storage. They are not transmitted anywhere unless you turn on background sync, below. Removing the extension deletes them — export a backup first if you want to keep them.

What Ocular requests over the network

To read a price, Ocular requests the product pages you have asked it to watch, from your own browser, using your existing session. It does not send those pages anywhere else.

Background sync

Background sync is on by default, so that prices keep being checked while your browser is closed. You can turn it off in the extension's options at any time, and you can point it at your own server instead of ours.

When it is on, Ocular sends that server the URLs and titles of the products you watch, plus a randomly generated device identifier. No prices, settings, or browsing history beyond those product URLs are sent.

That device identifier is generated in your browser and is not derived from anything about you or your machine. On its own it is not linked to your name or your email address. It becomes linked to them only if you turn on email price alerts — see below.

Optional account

Ocular works fully signed out, and nothing requires an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google handles the sign-in and tells us four things, which we store: a Google account identifier, your email address, your display name, and your profile picture URL. We never see your password.

An account does two things: it lets a watchlist follow you between your own browsers, and it lets you switch on email price alerts. Nothing else about what is stored for a product changes.

Email price alerts, and what linking costs

If a price drops while your browser is closed, no notification can reach you inside the browser. Email can — but only by knowing which watchlist is yours. Turning on email alerts therefore links your device's watchlist to your account, and we want to be exact about what that means rather than bury it.

No price record carries your account identifier; prices are still stored against the anonymous device identifier. But once linked, your account and that device identifier can be connected, and therefore so can the products and prices behind it. In plain terms: with email alerts on, the things you track are no longer anonymous to us.

What limits it:

  • It is off unless you turn it on. Signed out, or signed in without email alerts, nothing is linked.
  • It is reversible. Turning email alerts off unlinks the device, and the watchlist goes back to being anonymous.
  • Linking requires both your sign-in and your device at the same time, so it cannot be done on your behalf.
  • We use your address to send you price alerts about your own watchlist, and for nothing else. We do not sell or share it.

The mechanism is public — see the API contract, which documents the single endpoint that creates the link and what it writes.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not read pages on sites outside the retailers Ocular supports.
  • We do not collect payment details. Ocular is free and has no checkout.

Permissions

Ocular requests access only to the retail sites it supports, so it can read prices on product pages you open. It does not read pages on any other site.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes everything stored on your device. To delete an account and the four fields listed above, open an issue and we will remove it.
